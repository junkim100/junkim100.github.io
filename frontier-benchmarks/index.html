Boundaries before interpretation

Methodology and coverage

The corpus is a bounded review of named official indexes, launch materials, archive pages, and directly linked first-party technical sources.

Inclusive coverage window

Release reporting from 2024-01-01 through 2026-09-01 is reviewed. Included releases cover text and vision-language foundation models, reasoning and coding variants, previews, instruct variants, dated checkpoints, and dated point revisions.

Evidence threshold

Each occurrence requires a first-party source, revision state, precise locator, narrow evidence summary, setup disclosures, and review state. Comparison-table copies and third-party materials are excluded.

Careful absence language

“Not reported,” “dormant,” and “outdated” describe comparable sequences in reviewed public reporting only. Uncertain source coverage or lineage comparability pauses derivation instead of advancing an omission count.

Public coverage state

The fixed corpus contains 172 release coverage records and one quarantined candidate. Quarantine withholds an unresolved candidate from verified occurrences and does not predict what missing material would show.
