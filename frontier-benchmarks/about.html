About and limitations

Review window

The Observatory covers reviewed first-party public materials dated from 2024-01-01 through the fixed intake cutoff. Retrieval occurred later, so mutable pages may have changed.

This bounded intake does not prove complete source discovery, index closure, pagination closure, or semantic approval of every retained baseline record.

Source boundary

Occurrences require reviewed first-party public materials from the lab responsible for the release. The Observatory records whether those materials name a benchmark and links to the corresponding source when its stored destination passes the shared validation policy.

Unavailable and withheld sources remain limitations rather than evidence of non-reporting.

Score-free purpose

This is a score-free reporting record, not a ranking or comparison of benchmark results.

Recent counts are global retained-association counts. They are not performance measures or complete proof of evaluation activity.

Reading absence

An absence means only that a benchmark was not found in the reviewed public materials. It does not establish whether a lab evaluated the model privately or reported an evaluation elsewhere.

Derived lineage labels describe the recorded reporting sequence. Baseline coverage is not reapproved.

Generated coverage limitations

Coverage is bounded and incomplete. Missing or withheld records do not show that an evaluation was not run.

Generated audit counts require JavaScript; this static interpretation boundary remains valid without it.

Reporting definitions

Reporting definitions load from generated data.

The reporting labels describe reviewed public reporting within the same lineage, modality, and use class. They do not establish private evaluation activity or benchmark quality.
